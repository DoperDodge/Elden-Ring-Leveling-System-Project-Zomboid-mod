Tarnished - Elden Ring Leveling
Mod ID: ERLeveling

Full documentation, sandbox option reference and known issues:
  https://github.com/DoperDodge/Elden-Ring-Leveling-System-Project-Zomboid-mod

Quick start
  1. Open the character info window (default: C, or the new keybind, default P).
  2. The Health tab now has a rune counter along its bottom edge.
  3. The Runes tab is the level-up screen. Stage points with + / -, the mouse
     wheel or the arrow keys, then press LEVEL UP.
  4. You must be at a Site of Grace to spend runes: near a lit campfire,
     fireplace or stove, near a placed Grace Idol, or while resting.
